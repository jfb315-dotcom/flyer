## Accent color and placed items (20 Nov 2026)

- Accent defaults to white. White shows as white on photos/dark surfaces, charcoal on white/cream surfaces (accentOnLight), and lettering on any solid accent fill flips dark/white automatically (inkOn).
- Profiles saved while gold was the default move to white once (accentV: 2); a chosen color is kept.
- "Any color" rainbow button next to the swatches, with a hint line.
- Posts: the accent colors the headline, price tag, rules, stat icons, headshot ring, phone, panels, frame border and status tags, all larger. Printed flyers: status tags are about 35% bigger on all designs.
- Company logo is placed on the post like the cutout (own row under the preview, drag/pinch/corner dot). Saved as PNG so transparency survives. The flyer's own logo is left out of the Whole Flyer post when a logo is placed.
